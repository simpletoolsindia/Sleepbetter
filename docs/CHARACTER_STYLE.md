# Character style

The cast (Mochi, Ember the fox, Hoot the owl, Dozy the dinosaur, Koko the koala, Purr the cat) is drawn in code in `ui/components/Mochi.kt`.

## Look

The owner shared screenshots of popular kawaii sticker characters as a mood reference. We take only the general style, which is common to the whole kawaii genre and not owned by anyone:

- a big round head on a small body, with stubby arms and two feet
- a thick, warm dark-brown outline instead of black
- solid dot eyes with a white highlight, big blush cheeks, a small "ω" mouth
- flat colours with one soft shade underneath

## Copyright

The reference screenshots show **Bubu and Dudu** (officially Yier and Bubu), created by the Chinese artist Huang Xiao B and sold as licensed stickers and merchandise. Those characters, their names and the images are protected by copyright and trademark. So:

- None of the reference images are in this repository or the app, and they must not be added.
- We do not copy those characters: no white panda with black ears and a bow tie, no brown bear duo, not their names, poses, or faces.
- Our characters keep their own shapes and identity marks (Mochi is a rice-cake bun with a crescent-moon clip; the friends are a fox, owl, dinosaur, koala and cat).

Using a general art style is allowed; copying a specific character's expression is not. Using the real characters would need a written licence from the rights holder.
